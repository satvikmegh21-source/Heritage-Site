How this pipeline works

Capture

A photo, a note, and a GPS fix — pulled straight from the browser's real location and camera APIs. The GPS fix must be precise enough to prove you are on site; there is no fallback coordinate.

Working now

AI verification

Gemini checks that the photo is sharp, shows a heritage structure and matches the title you gave; your GPS position is checked against the ASI register entry for that site; the photo is compared with photos already on record (duplicates are dropped, new views are kept); and notes that aren't about the site are left out.

Working now

Moderation

Verified submissions wait here for a person to approve or reject them before they reach the public archive, with the verification results shown alongside.

Working now

Public map

Approved sites only, plotted with Leaflet over OpenStreetMap tiles.
